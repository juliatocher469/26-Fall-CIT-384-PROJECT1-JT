Welcome...

Ever wondered what goes bump in the night? What lies underneath your bed on sleepless nights? What creeps at you through your window curtain? The CRYPTIDS & GHOSTS web series will feed your curiousity of the mysterious and macabre. Join us, for we will take a deep dive into different types of ghosts and cryptids, starring Poltergeists, the Hat Man, and the Jersey Devil. Users will learn in depth stories on different accounts of these paranormal events across different states in Northern America. We will travel far and wide, from coast to coast and uncover some truly dark American history.

Background...

The United States was "discovered" in 1492, and was founded as a country on July 4th, 1776. Despite that, this country was inhabited by people who are indigenous to the land, dating further back than 15,000 years ago. Allegedly, the oldest account of a cryptid sighting in U.S. history is that of the Cape Ann Serpent in Massachusetts, spotted by writer and traveler John Josselyn in 1638.

Poltergeists

The movie Poltergeist (1982) popularized the premise of paranormal activity happening on ancient burial grounds, but this idea did not just come out of no where. There are personal accounts of haunts occuring on these grounds. Visit the Poltergeists page to learn more.
